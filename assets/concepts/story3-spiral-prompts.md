# Story 3 — the spiral uncurls and a sleigh crosses the moon (for ChatGPT, Work mode)

Attached: gothic-02.png (the full 941x1672 scene), and the full-canvas layers 02_moon.png,
03_spiral_hill_body_trees_fence.png and 04_spiral_tip.png (all 941x1672, same coordinates).

Task: make transparent animation art for two effects, in the same handcrafted gothic stop-motion
style, materials and lighting as the scene (dark navy textured material with a warm amber rim
light on the lower edge of the curl). Do not name or copy any existing film.

A. The hill's curl uncurling. Produce 5 PNG frames, each a full-canvas 941x1672 RGBA image holding
ONLY the curl (nothing else, everything else fully transparent), all anchored at the same base
point where the curl springs from the top of the hill (about x=560, y=211 in canvas coordinates,
exactly where layer 04 attaches). The base must stay fixed and seamless with layer 03:
 - curl_00: identical to the current layer 04 (the tightly curled spiral).
 - curl_01: loosened, the spiral opened by about one turn.
 - curl_02: about half unrolled, sweeping up and to the right.
 - curl_03: fully uncurled into one long, gently S-waving tapering tendril rising up and to the
   right toward the sky, about 450 px long, slightly rippled like stop-motion cloth, pointed tip.
 - curl_04: re-curling, a loose half spiral (so the animation can also play backwards).
 Keep line quality and texture consistent between frames; small texture changes frame to frame are
 welcome (it should look hand-animated).

B. A flying sleigh silhouette: one transparent PNG, tightly cropped, of a sleigh pulled by eight
reindeer in mid-flight, flying left to right, seen from the side, with a small figure in a hat
holding reins. A solid near-black silhouette with only a faint cool rim light along the top edge.
About 520 px wide. No text, family-friendly, charming and slightly spooky.

Deliver a zip with the PNGs, a manifest.json listing each file's name, canvas size and (for the
sleigh) pixel size, and a contact sheet; include a stack preview that composites curl_03 over layer
03 on the sky so the join can be checked. Verify the base of every curl frame lines up with layer 04's base.
